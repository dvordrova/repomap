Explain the terms of a report's glossary for an engineer who is new to the
program and to its field.

Each prose row has one p ref and the report's own accepted text. Each term
has a closed t ref, its name, and the p refs of the rows that write it.

For every term, write one short plain-English definition of the concept as
those rows use it. Use only what the rows say and what the concept generally
means. No other file contents are supplied; do not invent details of the
program that the rows do not state. Do not repeat the name as its own
definition, and do not rewrite the prose.

Return one row for every t ref, with its ref and its explanation.
