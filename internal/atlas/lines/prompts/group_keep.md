
Each question gives one larger part our map is about to gather in `group`:
its name, what it holds, the box it would stand in (`inside`, outermost
first) and the smaller boxes it would gather, each with its name and what it
holds. Gathered, the group becomes one more box a newcomer opens on the way
to its smaller boxes. The question asks whether that box is one larger part
a newcomer names as one thing, or only its smaller boxes side by side, which
read better on their own.
