# The gathering question's options

Each option of the question "Is `group` one larger part a newcomer names as
one thing, or only its smaller boxes side by side?" with its criteria: what
it is, what it includes, what it is not for, and examples. The request sends
them as each option's criteria.

## one part

What: The smaller boxes do one larger kind of work together, which the group's name and holds say in words of their own; a newcomer looks for that work and opens the group to find it.

Includes: the steps of one pipeline, the partners of one exchange, the variants of one thing, the layers of one store

Not for: a group whose name only joins its smaller boxes' names, whose holds only lists each box's own work, or whose boxes were put together because they were left over

Examples:
- "Order handling" over checkout, payment and shipping
- "Data storage" over the database models and the schema migrations
- "Public web APIs" over the shop's REST API and its GraphQL API

## only its boxes

What: The group's name and holds only put its smaller boxes side by side; they share no larger work a newcomer would name, so each reads better on its own.

Includes: a group named "A and B" after its boxes A and B; boxes left over after the others were gathered; unrelated boxes under one broad word

Not for: smaller boxes that do one larger kind of work together under a name of its own

Examples:
- "Search and e-mails" over the product search and the e-mail sender
- "Cart and logging" over the shopping cart and the log setup
- "Platform" over the settings loader, the image resizer and the admin panel
