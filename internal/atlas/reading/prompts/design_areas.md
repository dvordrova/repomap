# Propose the areas of a program's map

The input lists the parts already drawn on an architecture map, each with a
title and a purpose. Propose 4 to 8 larger areas around them. Divide along one
kind of line and use it for every area, so sibling areas are the same kind of
thing.

Only name the areas; a later step places every part in one.

For each area give a title of two to four words and one sentence of purpose,
in plain English, as one object whose keys are the titles.
