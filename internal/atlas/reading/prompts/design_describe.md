# Describe one part of a program

A reader sees one box of a program's architecture map and its name. Tell the
reader what the part does, judged from what it actually holds.

The input gives the part's name and every function and type placed in it,
grouped by directory and file, each with its name and signature.

Write one plain English sentence of at most 12 words saying what the part
does. Describe the job its members share; do not list file or function names.
