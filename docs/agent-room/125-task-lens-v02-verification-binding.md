# Decision: Task Lens v0.2 verification binding

Status: Approved by the repository owner through the explicit request to
continue after reviewing the real Task Lens v0.1 supervisor result.

## Baseline decision

Task Lens v0.1 is a technically valid but product-development `partial` result.
It passed decisive key-role recall (5/6), causal relation usefulness (4/6),
must-read recall (6/6), useful zero-call packs (2/3), source-scope safety, and
unsupported-claim safety. It failed exactly one declared gate: verification
usefulness reached 3/6 rather than the target 4/6.

The sealed v0.1 supervisor decision prohibits a new holdout and recommends
exactly one more bounded retrieval redesign focused on verification-frontier
binding.

## Product question

Given an already selected decisive relation, can one bounded local verification
probe retain and bind the repository-owned test, fixture, example, or documented
command that observes the relevant effect without weakening authority or
widening general retrieval?

## Predeclared hypothesis

The remaining verification loss is a binding problem, not a need for more
generic model prose. Exact receiver-method references, focused assertion-bearing
tests, generated fixtures, and repository-owned commands can be ranked against
the decisive endpoints and the task observable. This should lift at least one
currently failing verification episode to useful while preserving every v0.1
safety and cost gate.

## Approved implementation scope

1. Preserve the exact `tmp/task-lens-v01/` result as a read-only hashed baseline
   under a separate `tmp/task-lens-v02/` experiment root.
2. Keep the existing source-scope contract, task profiles, two decisive
   completion expansions, typed source relations, sufficiency gates, and
   zero-call route unchanged.
3. Strengthen exact local test binding for receiver methods and other
   structurally witnessed endpoint references.
4. Rank verification candidates by authority, exact endpoint/effect binding,
   observable assertion coverage, bounded graph/directory proximity, and task
   affinity. A benchmark cannot displace a focused test.
5. Protect decisive endpoints and key-role witnesses. The single verification
   probe may retain at most two verification anchors and replace only lower
   value filler.
6. Keep sibling-only or lexical-only candidates as `proposed_test_location`.
   `missing_evidence` and proposed locations never count as exact historical
   verification.
7. Derive bounded observable effects only from task input plus exact retained
   repository evidence. Record missing cases rather than pretending a nearby
   test covers them.
8. Re-run the same six known development episodes and the existing cheap-exit
   calibration with a frozen candidate, one process attempt per episode, no
   target commands, and no provider calls in the scored run.

## Frozen limits

- Initial candidates: 40.
- Retained anchors: 16; maximum 8 per file.
- Evidence files: 12; read bytes: 128 KiB; source scan: 4 MiB.
- Retained source: 128 KiB.
- Decisive completion expansions: 2.
- Verification probes: 1; verification anchors: 2.
- Local collector wall time: 10 seconds.
- Provider calls and semantic retries in the scored run: 0.
- Target-repository commands and tests: 0.

## Pass conditions

- Verification usefulness is at least 3 in at least 4 of 6 primary episodes.
- At least one of required/nullable, Accept/status, or multi-module release
  improves to at least 3, and no existing score at or above 3 regresses.
- False-exact or unrelated verification anchors: 0.
- Unsupported verification effects or invented commands: 0.
- v0.1 non-regression gates remain satisfied: decisive roles at least 5/6,
  must-read recall at least 5/6, decisive relation at least 4/6, useful
  zero-call at least 2/3, and zero major unsupported claims, unmarked clipping,
  or incomplete-scope absence claims.
- No budget, call, retry, or target-execution increase.

## Explicit non-goals

- No Project Input Contract, `.repomap.md`, company profile, or input-ablation
  work in this iteration.
- No new or untouched holdout and no fresh-generalization claim.
- No default-product integration or default `repomap` behavior change.
- No Fuego-specific production names, paths, symbols, aliases, or examples.
- No global call graph, pointer analysis, repository-wide SSA, embeddings,
  runtime-surface discovery, issue-tracker integration, or autonomous patching.
- No broad reranking redesign, new task profile, global budget increase,
  semantic retry, model-authored command, or weakened claim validation.
- No target-repository command or test execution.
- No commit or push.

## Completion condition

The iteration ends with an independently validated v0.2 supervisor decision and
exactly one next action. Passing may recommend preparing one untouched holdout,
but does not start it or authorize product integration. Failure stops repeated
tuning on the known set.
