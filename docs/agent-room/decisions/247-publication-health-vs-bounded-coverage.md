# 247 — Publication health is not bounded coverage completeness

**Status:** ACTIVE (owner-authorized through the linked report/mechanism
corrective goal after the fresh 37-repository corpus exposed a `37/37
DEGRADED` classifier collapse, 2026-08-09)

**Preserves:** Decisions 243–246 artifact integrity, stage diagnostics,
manifest verification, exact local remainder, bounded Study frontier and
mechanism failure isolation.

**Supersedes:** D243 and D246 only where they classified every accepted partial
or bounded-incomplete semantic result as a degraded publication.

## Causal evidence

The fresh exact-binary Age run completed all three semantic stages:

- Architecture accepted a partial model grouping covering 23/27 conceptual
  members and retained the other four in the exact local remainder;
- Study accepted 20/20 themes;
- Study investigation completed all eight planned cards and both batches.

The run was nevertheless labelled `DEGRADED`. Two coverage predicates caused
the false health result: a bounded Study request did not advertise the entire
local frontier, and the final shelf retained 20 materially distinct cards while
the readiness projection still compared it with the obsolete preferred maximum
of eight. The same conflation made all 37 valid fresh corpus publications
`DEGRADED`, so the status no longer distinguished actual product failure.

## Contract

Publication status describes whether the promised product surfaces were
successfully published and integrity-verified. It does not claim exhaustive
semantic coverage.

- `READY`: manifest/report JSON/report HTML verify; the Canvas is non-empty;
  Architecture is an accepted complete or accepted-partial model result; Study
  is accepted or accepted-partial with at least one published card; and any
  bound Study-investigation family is complete. Bounded frontier flags,
  retained exact remainder, missing-core diagnostics and portfolio preferences
  remain visible stage/report coverage facts, not health failures.
- `DEGRADED`: integrity is valid and the report remains usable, but a promised
  product stage is missing, empty, prepared-only, unavailable, failed,
  rejected/inconsistent, or a planned Study-investigation family is partial or
  failed.
- `FAILED`: required publication bytes or their manifest/integrity verification
  fail.

Architecture/Study console warnings and report diagnostics remain unchanged;
this decision removes no evidence and adds no provider call, retry, model
authority, report field or wire version.

## Acceptance

1. An Age-shaped 23/27 accepted-partial Architecture plus 20-card accepted
   bounded Study is `READY`.
2. Accepted-partial Study with non-empty exact cards is `READY`.
3. Empty, missing, unavailable and failed Architecture/Study states remain
   `DEGRADED`; integrity failures remain `FAILED`.
4. Partial/failed persisted Study investigation remains `DEGRADED`.
5. Corpus classification uses the same classifier and never derives health
   from file existence alone.
