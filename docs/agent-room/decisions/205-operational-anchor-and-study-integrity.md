# Decision 205: Operational anchor and Study integrity

## Status

Approved by the repository owner after fresh Casdoor product QA showed that
formally valid Study routes repeatedly described no-op `Start` methods and an
ACME challenge `CleanUp` method as background startup and service shutdown.
The owner requested root data/limit corrections rather than UI suppression.

## Outcome

Every local behavior anchor carries a producer-owned `proof_mode`:

- `process_entry` is an exact build-selected entry declaration;
- `call_target` is an exact locally observed static call boundary; and
- `declaration_family` is a repository declaration motif with no implied
  invocation, reachability, shared runtime role or complete family coverage.

Proof mode is independent of semantic anchor kind except that process-entry
kind and proof must agree. Display labels, limitations, filenames and method
names never determine proof mode downstream.

Global name/signature discovery may retain declaration-family motifs as local
opportunity or diagnostic evidence, but they cannot switch the D177 primary
Canvas to anchor-first operational grouping. A declaration becomes a Study
reading target only when an independent exact process-entry, call-target,
Surface, Navigator-selected local evidence or flow proof supports it. A
family-only declaration remains clickable source inventory but is not
advertised as an operational curriculum target. Historical anchor artifacts
without producer-owned proof mode are not reinterpreted from semantic kind;
they close to the package-grounded local Canvas.

## Truthful coverage

The old global-family first-32 collection and `members[:8]` projection are
removed. Complete declarations are deterministically grouped into bounded
anchor chunks. Architecture grounding v4 records considered and published
anchor, relationship and declaration-family-member counts, including unique
candidates rejected at the collection ceiling, plus a complete flag and closed
collection/persistence-limit reasons. Persisted bounds may produce a truthful
partial local grounding product; they cannot silently claim complete coverage
or erase the D177 report.

Declaration-family text must not claim that members share a signature unless
the producer actually checks exact compatible signatures. Current generic
families state only that exact declarations share a bounded local
classification.

## Study publication

- source drawers and Study target eligibility are separate projections;
- exact duplicate routes with the same sorted reading locator set publish once
  and remain hidden diagnostics rather than repeated curriculum cards;
- question-term diagnostics tokenize Unicode and match only exact local
  paths, symbols and saved source content, never model-authored labels or
  `what_to_look_for` prose; and
- lexical coverage remains a diagnostic, not semantic proof or authority.

If fewer than three operationally eligible, semantically distinct exact
reading roles remain, Study is honestly unavailable without a provider call.
It must not manufacture breadth from multiple lines in one generic lifecycle
family. Adding exact Beego/controller/auth/storage observations is a separate
producer extension after this correctness checkpoint.

## Acceptance

- no-op or unreachable name-only `Start`/`CleanUp` declarations are typed
  declaration-family and cannot become operational Study targets;
- a direct exact `service.Start` call target and process entry remain eligible;
- mixed signatures are not described as one proven shared signature;
- N/N+1 family input has complete chunks or explicit partial coverage, never a
  silent prefix;
- declaration-only motifs do not select anchor-first primary Canvas grouping;
- identical reading sets produce one visible route;
- Russian questions produce Unicode diagnostics without circular self-support;
  and
- all excluded declarations remain available through exact local source
  drawers and D177 facts.

No fuzzy semantic repair, model-prose parsing, runtime reachability claim,
source-byte provider input, retry, UI-only workaround, legacy reader or
migration is introduced.
