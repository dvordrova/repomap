response. The two-member `Запуск демона` component (`cmd/dockerd` plus exact
`cmd/dockerd.main`) displayed 31 runtime surfaces, including `/metrics` and
unrelated routes/tasks. `surfaceTriggerLocations` included the common process

ancestry as component participation.

The complete process-entry location remains in saved surface provenance.
Conceptual participation is narrower:

- a `process_entry` may join through its own process declaration/package;
- routes, servers, workers and other surfaces join through their own
  registration, descriptor, server-start, handler, evidence and frontier
  locations; and
- merely sharing one executable/process root never makes the entry component
  participate in every descendant surface.

This changes no discovery record, surface identity, provider payload, model
authority, runtime claim or source action.
