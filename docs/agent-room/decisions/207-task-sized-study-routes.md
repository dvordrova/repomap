# Decision 207: Task-sized Study routes

## Status

Approved by the repository owner's instruction to fix root limits after the
fresh Casdoor Study response produced five exact routes with reading counts
`[1,3,4,1,1]`, but the product hid three solely because every individual route
was required to contain at least three readings.

## Outcome

The Study catalog still requires at least three semantically eligible distinct
exact local reading locators before any provider call. That is the repository-
level breadth gate.

An individual Study route may contain one through five distinct exact reading
targets. Route size follows the task: a focused entrypoint, decoder or lifecycle
question may be answered by one exact target, while a cross-cutting question
may use several. Zero readings, more than five, duplicate targets, unknown or
wrong-kind refs, principal mismatch, unsupported prose and every existing
privacy/identity failure remain item-local rejection.

The prompt, live response resolver, persisted result/artifact validator,
provider-free replay and report reconstruction share the same `1..5` contract.
Earlier Study contract/cache identities miss closed. No route is padded,
merged, repaired or given invented evidence to satisfy a presentation count.

## Acceptance

- a catalog with fewer than three distinct eligible locators makes no provider
  call and publishes truthful Study-unavailable state;
- route cardinalities zero and six reject item-locally, while one and five
  resolve exactly;
- the saved Casdoor `[1,3,4,1,1]` response publishes all five routes when every
  other route check passes;
- exact duplicate reading sets remain deterministically hidden once;
- path, line, symbol, principal and evidence authority remain restored only
  from exact local target refs; and
- Architecture full, partial or rejected state does not change Study results.

No fuzzy repair, synthetic padding, semantic retry, source-byte provider input,
raw Orientation, UI-only workaround, legacy reader or migration is introduced.
