# Decision 200: Architecture short refs and truthful Atlas Study contract

## Status

Approved by the repository owner in the current supervisory session after two
live Decision 199 Casdoor runs proved both sides of the parity bridge: the
local Decision 177 Canvas remained intact, while model Architecture acceptance
was sample-dependent and Atlas Study could generate useful prose but lose the
whole product on a wrong-kind reference.

## Outcome

Decision 199 remains the Atlas-first product sequence. This decision hardens
its two provider contracts without restoring raw Orientation, old semantic
fan-out, fuzzy repair, sharding, migration or a UI branch.

Architecture now sends private request-local typed refs for every advertised
candidate, member, anchor, flow and relation identity. Canonical opaque IDs,
paths, symbols, provenance, catalog hashes and backend versions remain local.
Refs are allocated deterministically after reserving every canonical opaque
value, share one collision-free namespace, and are resolved by exact kind and
request identity only. Every provider-wire identity field is checked before
marshal. Exact canonical opaque tokens are removed from model-visible labels;
ordinary near-match prose remains unchanged. Unknown, wrong-kind, colliding,
raw-canonical and cross-request refs fail closed without choose-first or string
repair.

The Decision 177 local ArchitectureCanvas remains the canonical visible base
map. Model acceptance gates grouping and prose enrichment only. Rejection,
missing enrichment or a non-resource semantic failure never hides or mutates
local members, relations, facts, anchors or Surfaces. The current exclusive
membership rule remains a known contract limitation; this decision does not
introduce primary/related multi-membership.

Architecture and Atlas Study each own one non-contradictory report-language
instruction. The generic client no longer adds a competing canonical-English
instruction to these stage-owned requests. Request, cache, replay and saved
record identities bind the exact body and language.

Atlas Study exposes an explicit typed `brief_support_choices` allowlist for
subsystem, component, Surface, reading target, evidence and document refs.
Unit refs may remain route-principal context but cannot be selected as Brief
support. Brief refs and each route are resolved independently by exact kind;
one invalid route preserves a valid Brief and valid sibling routes. The
provider DTO contains no catalog ref, version, hash or backend-owned token.
The prompt advances to v2, invalidating the earlier wire/cache identity.

## Runtime evidence

Architecture status advances to v4 and records the exact sent request,
request/response bytes, candidate/member/anchor cardinality, provider usage
presence and tokens, finish reason, completion state, attempts and a closed
registry of current validation diagnostics. Validation occurs before accepted
cache or artifact writes. Resource exhaustion remains the Decision 194 typed
terminal path and is not converted into a status diagnostic or fallback.

The saved 14:44 Casdoor Atlas Study response is a provider-free regression
fixture. Its original wrong-kind unit Brief support is rejected exactly. With
valid Brief support refs, three valid sibling routes survive while three bad
routes receive bounded independent diagnostics. The etcd-sized Architecture
gate records input cardinality and bytes without claiming a new live completion
sample.

## Acceptance

- exact short refs are collision-free, deterministic under reordered local
  input and privately bound to the request/cache identity;
- no current provider-wire identity field can carry a canonical opaque ID;
- unknown binding anchors produce neither request bytes nor a cache key;
- model grouping never changes local facts or relations, and local Canvas is
  visible without accepted enrichment;
- Navigator-empty fixtures still construct Architecture and Study;
- Russian and English requests contain exactly one stage-owned language
  contract;
- wrong-kind Brief refs fail closed and one bad route preserves valid siblings;
- status v4 accepts only its 33 currently produced diagnostic codes; and
- no legacy Orientation, Guided Tour, localization or Study review-fan-out call
  enters the ordinary Atlas-first path.

One owner-run live Casdoor/etcd comparison after installing the exact binary is
the product gate. Live provider calls are never made by the supervisor or
source agents.
