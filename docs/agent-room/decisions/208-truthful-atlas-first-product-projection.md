# Decision 208: Truthful Atlas-first product projection

## Status

Approved explicitly by the repository owner after review of the provider-free
implementation, the fresh cache-backed Casdoor report and the independent
contract verdict. It does not change a provider request or response contract.

## Outcome

The complete D177 local Canvas remains the canonical architecture base. Exact
local non-package structural relations are published at member level even when
either endpoint participates in zero, one or several conceptual components.
The relation witness is the single canonical endpoint identity. Sorted plural
component participants are a derived presentation projection; singular
component endpoints exist only when exact membership leaves one choice. The
report never chooses by order and never expands a plural relation into a
Cartesian set of invented component edges.

Atlas Study references a local D177 component, while the visible Architecture
may use independently accepted model component IDs. A Study navigation target
is therefore restored only through exact typed member intersection with the
visible Canvas: zero matches means no focus, one match means one related
navigation target, and several matches mean no singular focus. The bridge is
related navigation, not ownership or component equivalence.

The default product presentation is consolidated:

- the complete accepted Repository Brief is rendered once on Overview,
  including its five statements and supported domain terms;
- Study starts with reading routes and does not repeat the Brief;
- empty Integrations are not rendered;
- the local unclassified-by-model remainder is excluded from conceptual
  component grids and shown as a separate explained coverage disclosure with
  exact source actions where available;
- package inventory is a compact disclosure with an exact shared prefix and
  package-declaration source actions; stripped static reports use commit-pinned
  links instead of inert drawer buttons; and
- user-facing component cards use exact local names and useful code/source
  context rather than opaque IDs, internal anchor/member counts or whole-card
  link decoration.

The Architecture Canvas and Atlas Study report projection versions are bumped
because their published shapes and navigation semantics change. Earlier static
reports remain self-contained and are not reinterpreted or migrated.

## Acceptance

- an exact member relation with a plural endpoint remains present once, has no
  chosen singular endpoint and produces no Cartesian component edges;
- relation-only components retain the relation in their inspector, with names
  resolved from exact typed candidate IDs rather than name matching;
- Study local and visible component IDs may differ; exact zero/one/many member
  intersections yield no focus/one related focus/no focus respectively;
- the full Brief appears exactly once on Overview and none of its fields remain
  on Study;
- empty Integrations and the local remainder do not appear as ordinary product
  component cards, while the remainder's exact local source remains available;
- package declaration actions work both with an embedded snippet and in a
  stripped commit-pinned static report; and
- provider-free report tests, the complete Go test suite and `go vet ./...`
  remain green.

No provider call shape, Study route cardinality, target shelf, model authority,
fuzzy repair, choose-first ownership, raw Orientation, legacy reader or
migration is introduced. A diverse typed Study target shelf and typed
first-contact span rule require a later separately approved decision; observed
roles must be producer-owned and repository-shaped rather than a fixed Casdoor
lane checklist.
