# Decision 206: Exact partial Architecture enrichment

## Status

Approved by the repository owner's instruction to continue root-cause product
corrections after two independent complete provider responses omitted exactly
one requested conceptual member (49/50 and 55/56). The D177 local Canvas is
already the canonical complete base; treating model coverage as an atomic
semantic safety invariant made useful enrichment depend on provider checklist
compliance rather than identity or authority correctness.

## Outcome

An Architecture response may be accepted as an exact partial model projection.
Every returned member and anchor ref must still resolve exactly within the
request-local typed catalog. Unknown, wrong-kind, private/canonical,
cross-request or duplicate-within-component refs, malformed structure,
resource exhaustion and invalid authority remain closed failures.

Uncovered conceptual members are computed locally as the exact deterministic
set difference between the complete requested conceptual set and distinct
accepted model memberships. They are materialized once in an explicitly local
`unclassified_by_model` remainder group. The group has no model-authored name,
ownership, hypothesis, relation or inferred association. Structural locators
remain complete local context outside model coverage. Many-to-many conceptual
membership remains valid.

There is no percentage or minimum-coverage threshold. At least one valid model
component is required for partial enrichment; zero covered members or any
identity/shape failure rejects the model projection and leaves the D177 Canvas
untouched. Final publication always represents every requested conceptual
member either in one or more exact model memberships or in the local remainder.

Status and artifacts distinguish full and partial acceptance. They persist the
requested conceptual count, covered distinct count, membership occurrence
count, uncovered count and exact uncovered identities, plus an explicit
`accepted_partial` outcome. Partial results are never reported as complete.
Accepted partial cache entries retain the exact request and original response
identity and are revalidated under the new contract; earlier exact-coverage
records miss closed.

## Authority

The provider cannot grant operational authority. A model `hypothesis` value is
removed from the wire or treated as non-authoritative input and never copied as
the product verdict. Local resolution derives component hypothesis status only
from exact producer-owned proof:

- `declaration_family` is static motif/grouping evidence and never removes
  hypothesis status;
- no anchors means the component remains hypothetical; and
- a component can be non-hypothetical only when qualifying `process_entry` or
  `call_target` proof is scoped exactly to every member for which the
  component-wide status would make an operational claim. Mixed or partial
  proof leaves the whole component hypothetical while retaining the exact
  evidence separately.

Model grouping never changes local D177 facts, relations, structural locators,
surface ownership or flow bindings. Study remains independent of Architecture
being fully accepted, partially accepted or rejected and continues to use
exact local typed locators and anchors.

## Acceptance

- the saved 55/56 Casdoor response is accepted partial, with only `service`
  placed in one deterministic local remainder and all 56 conceptual members
  represented in the final Canvas;
- an exact 56/56 response is accepted full with no remainder;
- 0/N, malformed, unknown, wrong-kind, private or duplicate-within-component
  responses reject without changing the D177 Canvas;
- cross-component repeated exact membership remains valid;
- declaration-family-only, unanchored or partially grounded components remain
  hypothetical even when the provider claims otherwise;
- exact qualifying process-entry/call-target proof can ground only its scoped
  component members;
- ordering does not change covered/uncovered identities, local remainder IDs or
  cache identity; and
- Study result/status is invariant across full, partial and rejected
  Architecture enrichment.

No fuzzy repair, choose-first ownership, semantic retry, silent clipping,
source-byte provider input, raw Orientation, UI-only workaround, legacy reader
or migration is introduced.
